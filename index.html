🔍 Botanical & Fungal Morphology

Concentric growth bands alternating in shades of buff, cinnamon, and cream with a velvet-soft upper surface. Underside features 3–8 microscopic white pores per millimeter rather than gills.

✋ Hands-in-the-Dirt Tactile Test

Run your thumb across the damp surface: true Turkey Tail feels like soft suede or moleskin, and the underside is flat and spongy. If the underside is smooth parchment with no pores, it is False Turkey Tail (Stereum ostrea).

🌱 Garden & Soil Regeneration Role

Saprophytic log inoculant: Highly beneficial for building woodland fungal humus and accelerating nurse-log soil replenishment in shaded garden corners.

⚡ 100% Open-Source Edge Intelligence Stack

Understory does not call OpenAI, Anthropic, or proprietary cloud APIs. Every model is open-weight and compiled directly for low-power edge silicon via Georgi Gerganov's C/C++ runtimes. It is fundamentally impossible for network dropouts on the trail to interrupt your experience.

Speech Ingestion

whisper.cpp

Quantized OpenAI Whisper (`ggml-base.en.bin` Q5_1). Transcribes voice whisper inputs in under 180ms with 0 internet connection on ARM Cortex-A76.

Vision-Language Model

Moondream2 (1.86B)

4-bit quantized vision model. Evaluates leaf venation, fungal pores, and insect markings with sub-400ms inference on consumer mobile hardware.

Reasoning & Taxonomy Core

Llama 3.2 3B-Instruct

Meta's open-weight model executed via `llama.cpp` (Q4_K_M). Specialized system prompt generates sensory-first, tactile outdoor guidance instead of screen text.

Local RAG Knowledge

LlamaIndex + SQLite

Indexed USDA plant taxonomy, Audubon bird calls, and OpenStreetMap topography using quantized `bge-micro-v2` embeddings stored on-device.

Neural Speech Synthesis

Piper TTS (ONNX)

Lightweight local neural text-to-speech running `en_US-lessac-medium` at >6x real-time speed. Whispers warm, measured guidance directly into headphones.

Air-Gapped Privacy

Zero Cloud Telemetry

No telemetry, no tracking pixels, no server pings. Sensitive wild foraging spots and endangered native plants remain strictly private on the user's hardware.
